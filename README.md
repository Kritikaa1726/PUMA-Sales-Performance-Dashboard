#  PUMA Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The PUMA Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides a detailed analysis of sales performance, revenue, profitability, product categories, and regional sales.

The dashboard features a modern black-and-red theme inspired by PUMA's brand identity, with interactive visuals and filters to make business insights easy to understand.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Evaluate regional sales contributions.
- Monitor monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Overview
Displays key business metrics, including total revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin.

### 2. Top 10 Best-Selling Products
Highlights the top-performing products based on sales.

### 3. Sales by Product Category
Provides a category-wise comparison of Footwear, Apparel, and Accessories.

### 4. Sales by Region
Analyzes sales distribution across East, West, North, and South regions.

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend
Visualizes monthly revenue and profit trends to understand business performance over time.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Allows users to explore the dashboard using:
- Region
- Category
- Month
- Sales Channel
